Formula

Space heating usage = annual kWh × 97% (gas cooking)

Space heating cost = space heating usage × (unit rate p/kWh ÷ 100)

Condensing-mode saving = 1 − η(75°C flow) / η(selected flow)

η comes from the efficiency graph using return = flow − 20°C. Factory 75°C flow is about 55°C return, just above dew point (non-condensing). 70°C flow is about 50°C return, just into condensing, so it still saves. Lower flow keeps climbing the green curve, with smaller extra gains at the coldest returns. HHIC’s 6–8% for 75°C → 55°C flow matches this curve. Standing charge is not included.
