Boiler, controls and TRVs: Vaillant ecoTEC 624 with Honeywell

A 3-bedroom house with an open-vent heating system and a hot-water cylinder. The boiler was replaced with a Vaillant system boiler, Honeywell S Plan controls, and TRVs in place of the manual radiator valves.

24 October 2019

Job overview

A 3-bedroom house with an open-vent heating system and a hot-water cylinder had its boiler replaced. The boiler was a Vaillant system boiler. Honeywell S Plan controls. The manual radiator valves were upgraded with TRVs.

The system was converted to a sealed heating system with a system boiler. New controls were installed, as well as new wiring. The system was powerflushed and the radiator valves upgraded.

Components used

  • Vaillant ecoTEC Plus 624 system boiler
  • ST9400C programmer
  • T6360 room thermostat
  • L641 cylinder thermostat
  • Honeywell zone valves
  • Altecnic Ecocal TRV sets

Post-completion review

The customer was inclined towards a Vaillant 630 boiler. Using our experience we convinced them not to oversize the boiler, so we settled on a 624. Our calculations suggested even a 618 would have been more than enough, but sometimes people’s perceptions override a more correct solution because of fear. See what size boiler.

The preference is to have domestic hot water priority. In this property there will be tenants, and the landlord asked for everything to be kept as simple as possible.

Switching to an Altecnic Ecocal TRV is a solid and reliable valve. The seating can be replaced on a live system. See our TRV valve replacement article.
