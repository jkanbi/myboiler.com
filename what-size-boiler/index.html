This is not a heat-loss calculation. UK bands like these are used for a first look only. Insulation, ceiling height, water pressure, and how many outlets run at once all change the right kW. Oversizing a combi for DHW is common; heating-only and system boilers are usually smaller for the same house because hot water comes from a cylinder.
