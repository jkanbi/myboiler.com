How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Potterton Promax Combi

Set the heating flow with the radiator knob (18). The video takes it from 75°C down to 55°C.

This fascia has a reset selector, a round temperature display, a radiator knob (18) and a tap knob (19). It is the usual layout on Promax Combi 24, 28 and 33. Main Eco uses the same family of controls. It is not the later two-knob LCD Duo-tec style.

  1. Knob 18 with the radiator symbol is heating flow. Knob 19 with the tap is hot water — leave that if you only came to lower the heating.
  2. Turn the radiator knob until the display shows the flow you want. Typical range is about 30°C to 80°C.
  3. Stop around 55°C if the house still warms. Add time on the programmer if rooms take longer to come up.
