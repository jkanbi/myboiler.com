How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Potterton Boiler

The usual Potterton Promax Combi fascia has a reset selector, a round display and a radiator knob. Typical target is about 55°C if the house still warms.

Promax Combi

Reset selector, round display, and a radiator knob. Knob 18 is heating.

If this panel is not yours, look the model up on Boiler Manuals.

When the flow is set, go back to the Boiler Optimiser to log what it was and what you set.
