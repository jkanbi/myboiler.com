Our mission

MyBoiler.com helps you run heating and hot water with less waste. A clear fault, a lower flow temperature, or the right replacement saves gas and money. That is the point of the site.

What you will find here

  • Guides for boilers, controls, OpenTherm, and heat pumps.
  • Fault codes, so a display code has a plain meaning.
  • Tools, including the boiler optimiser and the heat pump checker.
  • A quote when the work needs a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Most of a home’s energy goes on heating and hot water. The first saving is usually the system you already have: the right flow temperature, a control that can modulate, and a repair before a replacement. A heat pump is the larger step where the radiators and the house suit it.

What this site does not do

These pages do not install a boiler, a heat pump, or solar panels. The building we work from is described on the carbon emissions page. Advice here is guidance. Installation and repairs stay with a qualified engineer.
