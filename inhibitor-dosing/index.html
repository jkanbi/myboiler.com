Method

Fernox F1 is commonly 0.5 L per 100 L. Sentinel X100 is 1 L per 100 L. Always read the bottle — pack sizes and treat volumes change.
BS 7593:2019 requires the system to be dosed to the maker’s concentration, then tested. Do not mix brands in the same system.

Guidance only. Follow the inhibitor maker’s label. Over-dosing some products is wasteful; under-dosing fails BS 7593. Drain-down or a failed test means re-dose, not a one-off fill.
