Boiler Cover

Boiler cover is usually taken out for peace of mind and to avoid a large bill when something fails. Many homeowners still buy the wrong level of protection — or pay twice for cover they already have.

Check what you already have

New boilers usually carry a manufacturer warranty. Taking out extra boiler cover during that period is often money wasted, unless the plan adds something the warranty does not (for example a guaranteed annual service, or heating-system parts the maker does not cover). Keep the warranty valid: annual service by a Gas Safe engineer, inhibitor, and the installer’s benchmark paperwork all matter.

Some home-insurance policies already include boiler or home-emergency cover. Read the schedule before you buy a second plan.

Self-insuring

Another option is to put aside a regular amount for repairs and a replacement, instead of paying a monthly plan. That needs discipline — the pot has to stay for heating, not other spending — and you still need a Gas Safe engineer when it breaks. A well installed, optimised, and maintained system is less likely to fail in the first place. See boiler service and repairs.

Read exclusions before you pay. Pre-existing faults, limescale, sludge, shower pumps, and non-natural-gas appliances are commonly refused. Cover is not a reason to skip servicing.

YourRepair

YourRepair (YourCare Repairs Ltd) is a UK boiler and home-care company. Their boiler cover is a service and support agreement, not an insurance policy: it is built around an annual boiler service, 24/7 support, and access to a network of Gas Safe engineers for repairs on the items in your plan.

Plans can include unlimited repairs with parts and labour for covered faults, with a choice of call-out excess. They cover domestic natural-gas boilers across most of the UK; some makes and all LPG, oil, electric, back, and dual-purpose boilers are excluded. Check the live plan list and their service agreement for what is in and what is not, including beyond-economical-repair rules.
