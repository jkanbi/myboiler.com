Boiler repair

A few checks outside the case can restore heat. Anything on the gas appliance itself stays with a Gas Safe registered engineer. The old hub page that treated wiring inside a boiler as a mid-level DIY job was wrong — do not follow it.

A boiler is a gas appliance. In the UK, work on gas fittings and gas appliances must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. That includes opening the case, working on electrical parts inside the boiler, changing a fan or PCB, and anything on the gas valve or flue. A YouTube video is not a qualification.

If you can smell gas, hear a leak, or a carbon monoxide alarm is sounding, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

This page sits next to the shorter first-checks list on boiler repairs. Use that when you want a same-day call-out. Use this page when you are deciding what kind of repair it is, whether a manufacturer cap helps, and when a new boiler is the cheaper end of the story.

What you can check without opening the boiler

These do not involve the gas path. If they restore heat, you may not need a visit.

  • Confirm the programmer or thermostat is calling for heat, and that TRVs on radiators are open.
  • Read the display. A lockout or fault code is useful for the engineer — see fault codes A–Z and combi boiler problems.
  • Check sealed-system pressure. Many combis want about 1–1.5 bar when cold. Guide: boiler pressure. If you are not sure how the filling loop works, leave it.
  • Use the manufacturer reset only as the instructions describe. Repeated resets without a repair can mask a real fault.
  • Frozen condensate pipes are common in cold weather. Thawing from the outside with warm water is a homeowner job; do not use boiling water on plastic pipe.
  • A broken removable control knob, if it is only a clip-on plastic part on the outside of the case, can sometimes be replaced from a merchant. Stop if the case has to come off.

A loose cable inside the boiler is not a mid-level DIY job. It is live equipment next to a gas appliance. That is a Gas Safe visit, not a video-and-screwdriver afternoon.

When to call a Gas Safe engineer

Call if there is no heat or hot water after those checks, a persistent fault code, a leak from the boiler, ignition failure, a smell of fumes, or you are not confident topping up pressure. Same-day attendance matters most in winter, for vulnerable occupants, or where a leak is causing damage.

A specialist who stocks that brand often diagnoses faster than a generalist who has to order the part. Ask for a Gas Safe registration number, a written parts-and-labour figure, and whether the parts are genuine OEM.

Manufacturer fixed-price repairs

Several makers sell a one-off out-of-guarantee repair: one published fee for the visit, labour, and most boiler parts. That can beat a time-and-parts quote when two major parts fail on the same visit. It is usually the wrong buy for a cheap sensor, a condensate freeze, or a pressure top-up.

The old hub used stale pound figures (a pump or fan at £150, labour at £180, a £250 “saving”). Those numbers are not safe to republish. Current manufacturer fees, exclusions, and official booking links are on fixed-price boiler repairs, checked against the maker pages in October 2026.

If the appliance is still in manufacturer guarantee and the terms have been kept (annual service, Benchmark paperwork, registered installer), the repair should go through the warranty, not a paid package. Say so when you book.

Repair versus replacement

A repair that keeps an old boiler going for another winter is often the right spend. Replacement starts to win when:

  • The same boiler is failing every few months, and the time at home for each visit is itself a cost.
  • The engineer already knows a heat exchanger, or another structural part some maker packages exclude, is on the way out.
  • The appliance is out of guarantee, parts are scarce, and a new boiler would come with a long manufacturer warranty that still needs an annual service.

A new combi, as a straight swap, is typically quoted as one fitted price. Bands and what should be in that price are on combi boiler cost. For a written quote from a Gas Safe engineer, use quote.
