How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Warmflow Agentis Combi

The combi screen has seven buttons. Central heating is CH+ and CH−, not the hot-water pair.

  1. From the home screen, press CH+ or CH−.
  2. The setpoint runs from 1 to 9. 1 is nominally about 50°C and 9 is nominally about 82°C.
  3. Wait about 5 seconds after the last press. That is when it is saved.

Mode cycles through central heating and hot water, hot water only, central heating only, and off. The information button shows the live flow temperature. It does not change the setpoint. If an outside sensor is fitted, the information screen can also show the weather-compensated setpoint.

Look up your exact model on Boiler Manuals if the fascia does not match. Then return to the Warmflow flow-temperature page or the Boiler Optimiser.
