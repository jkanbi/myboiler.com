How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Warmflow Agentis Heat-Only Boiler

Heat-only Agentis boilers use a control thermostat knob, not the combi’s CH+ and CH− buttons.

  1. Turn the control knob clockwise to raise the radiator temperature.
  2. Turn it anticlockwise to lower it. The knob is user adjustable from nominally 45°C to 80°C.

A manual-reset safety cut-off sits under a lock-out cover. That reset is not the flow setting.

Look up your exact model on Boiler Manuals if the fascia does not match. Then return to the Warmflow flow-temperature page or the Boiler Optimiser.
