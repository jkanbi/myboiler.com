Energy

Energy use in a UK home is mostly heating and hot water. This page is a short directory: who runs the wires and the gas pipe, and which MyBoiler tools split a bill into heating versus the rest.

The old hub page talked about a move to sustainable energy, Tesla Powerwall calculators, and a gas-and-electric consumption calculator. The Powerwall tools were never brought across to this site, so they are not linked here. Gas and electricity use is covered by the calculators below. Practical savings sit on money and energy saving. How we heat our own office is on carbon emissions.

Find who runs the pipes and wires

Your supplier is who you pay. Your network operator owns the cables or the gas pipes. They are different companies. You need the network operator for a power cut, a new connection, or a meter move — not a bill query.

  • Electricity network operator (DNO / IDNO) — Energy Networks Association postcode finder. In England, Scotland and Wales you can also call 105 in a power cut.
  • Gas supplier, MPRN and transporter — findmysupplier.energy (Xoserve). If you can smell gas, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.
