How to Pair/Re-pair Hive 2 Heating Devices

Step-by-step guide to pairing and re-pairing your Hive 2 heating devices. Understand what the receiver and hub lights mean during the pairing process.

Before You Start

Pairing your Hive 2 devices connects the receiver to your Hive Hub, allowing you to control your heating remotely. You may need to pair or re-pair your devices if you've replaced a component, reset your system, or if your devices have lost connection.

What You'll Need

  • Hive Hub (plugged in and connected to your router)
  • Hive Receiver (installed and powered)
  • Access to the Hive app on your smartphone

Pairing Process

Step 1: Put the Receiver into Pairing Mode

  1. Locate the pairing button on your Hive Receiver (usually a small button marked with a circular arrow icon)
  2. Press and hold the pairing button for approximately 10 seconds
  3. The receiver lights will start flashing - refer to the table below to understand the light patterns

Step 2: Pair via the Hive App

  1. Open the Hive app on your smartphone
  2. Go to Menu → Install & Manage Devices → Install a Device
  3. Select "Heating" then follow the on-screen instructions
  4. The app will search for your receiver and complete the pairing process

Step 3: Verify Connection

Once paired successfully, both the receiver and hub lights should show solid green. You should now be able to control your heating through the Hive app.

Hive Receiver Light Indicators

The lights on your Hive Receiver indicate its current status. Here's what each pattern means:

Light Pattern Colour Meaning Action Required
Solid Green Receiver is paired and working normally None - system is operating correctly
Flashing (slow) Green Receiver is in pairing mode Complete pairing in the Hive app
Flashing (fast) Amber Receiver is searching for the Hub Ensure Hub is powered and connected
Solid Amber Weak signal or connection issue Move Hub closer or check for interference
Solid Red Receiver has lost connection to Hub Check Hub is online, may need re-pairing
Flashing Red Receiver fault or overheating protection Turn off power, wait 5 minutes, then restart. Contact support if persists
Off No light No power to receiver Check power supply and circuit breaker

Hive Hub Light Indicators

The Hive Hub also has indicator lights that show its connection status:

Light Pattern Colour Meaning Action Required
Solid Green Hub is online and connected None - system is working correctly
Flashing Green Hub is starting up or updating Wait for process to complete (up to 5 minutes)
Solid Amber Hub has no internet connection Check ethernet cable and router connection
Flashing Amber Hub is connecting to the internet Wait a few moments for connection to establish
Solid Red Hub fault or registration issue Unplug for 30 seconds, reconnect. Contact support if persists
Flashing Red Hub firmware update failed Contact Hive support for assistance
Purple Purple Hub is in pairing mode Pair devices through the Hive app

Troubleshooting Pairing Issues

Devices Won't Pair

  • Check distances: Ensure the Hub and Receiver are within 30 metres of each other with minimal obstructions
  • Remove interference: Keep devices away from other wireless equipment, microwaves, or thick walls
  • Restart both devices: Power cycle both the Hub and Receiver
  • Check app version: Ensure you're using the latest version of the Hive app

Connection Keeps Dropping

  • Signal strength: Move the Hub closer to the Receiver or use a Hive Signal Booster
  • Hub location: Place the Hub in a central location away from electronic interference
  • Check for updates: Ensure all Hive devices have the latest firmware

Reset to Factory Settings

If all else fails, you can reset your receiver to factory settings:

  1. Press and hold the pairing button on the receiver for 20 seconds
  2. All lights will flash simultaneously
  3. Release the button - the receiver is now reset
  4. Follow the pairing process from the beginning

Additional Tips

  • Always ensure your Hub has a stable internet connection before pairing
  • Keep your Hive app updated to the latest version
  • If you have multiple Hive devices, pair them one at a time
  • Write down your Hive account details for future reference

Still Having Issues?

If you're still experiencing problems after following this guide, contact Hive support directly through the app or visit their support website. They can provide specific troubleshooting for your setup and remotely diagnose connection issues.
