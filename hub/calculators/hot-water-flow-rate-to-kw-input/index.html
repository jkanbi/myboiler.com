Hot Water Flow Rate to KW Input

Calculate the required heat input (KW) based on hot water flow rate and desired temperature rise.

Flow Rate (LPM) 10.0

24.42
kilowatts (KW)

Formula Reference

KW = (LPM / 60) × ΔT × 4.186

LPM = Flow rate in litres per minute
ΔT = Temperature rise in degrees Celsius
4.186 = Specific heat capacity of water (kJ/kg·K)
60 = Conversion from LPM to LPS

Important: This calculator provides indicative heat input requirements only. Always consult with a qualified heating engineer for your specific installation requirements.
