Metric Instructions

  1. Ensure that all other gas burning appliances are turned off
  2. Turn on the appliance to be rated to its maximum setting – allow to run for 10 minutes
  3. Take the first reading from the meter (R1)
  4. At exactly 2 minutes take the second reading (R2)

Note: You multiply by 30 because your 2 min read converts to gas units used in 1 hour.

Formula Reference

Gross KW = (((Finish-Start)/1000 × 3600 × 10.76) / Timer)
Gross m³/h = Gross KW / 10.76
Net KW = Gross KW / 1.11
Net m³/h = Gross KW / 10.76 / 1.11
CV = Calorific value 10.76 kW
Factor = Gross to Net factor 1.11

Imperial Instructions

  1. Ensure that all other gas burning appliances are turned off
  2. Turn on the appliance to be rated to its maximum setting – allow to run for 10 minutes
  3. Time in seconds how long it takes for test dial to complete 1 revolution (1ft³)

Note: ft³ × 0.028 = m³

Formula Reference

Gross KW = (((1×3600×1040)/Time/3412)×Dial)
Gas Rate m³/h = Gross KW / 10.76
Net KW = Gross KW / 1.11
Net m³/h = Gross KW / 10.76 / 1.11
CV = Calorific value 1040 BTU
3412 = Converts BTU to kW
Factor = Gross to Net factor 1.11
