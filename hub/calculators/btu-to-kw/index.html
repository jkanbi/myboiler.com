About BTU to Watts Conversion

Conversion Formula: 1 BTU/h = 0.29307107 Watts

BTU (British Thermal Unit) is a traditional unit of heat energy commonly used in heating and cooling specifications, particularly in the UK and US. Watts and kilowatts are the metric SI units of power used in modern heating system specifications.

Common Uses:

  • Converting boiler ratings from BTU to kW
  • Comparing radiator outputs across different specification formats
  • Understanding heat pump capacity ratings
  • Verifying heating system calculations

Quick Reference:

  • 10,000 BTU/h ≈ 2.93 kW
  • 24,000 BTU/h ≈ 7.03 kW (typical combi boiler)
  • 30,000 BTU/h ≈ 8.79 kW
  • 40,000 BTU/h ≈ 11.72 kW
