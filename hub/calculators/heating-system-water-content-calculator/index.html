Formula

Water Content (L) = (Length mm / 1000) × Type Factor

Length = Radiator length in millimeters
Type Factor = Water content factor based on radiator type and height

Type factors range from 2.8 (Type 11 - 300mm) to 9.9 (Type 21/22 - 700mm)

Important: This calculator provides approximate water content for center radiators. Actual values may vary by manufacturer. Use for system volume calculations and chemical inhibitor dosing. Always consult radiator specifications and a qualified heating engineer.
