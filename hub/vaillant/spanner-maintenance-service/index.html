Spanner Maintenance Service

Understanding the Vaillant spanner symbol and what it means when your boiler displays the maintenance service icon. Learn how to access the Live Monitor for detailed service information.

Symbol Name Description
Maintenance / Service required In the "Live monitor", you can read further information about the reason for the service

If the open-ended spanner is displayed, the boiler requires a service.

Consult your competent person about doing this. The boiler is not in fault mode but continues to operate.

In the "Live monitor", you can read further information about the reason for the service

Status Codes

Status codes Meaning Visibility
S 00 Heating mode: No heat demand Displays in Heating mode
S 02 Heating mode: Water pump running
S 03 Heating mode: Ignition sequence Displays in Heating mode
S 04 Heating mode: Burner ignited Displays in Heating mode
S 06 Heating mode: Fan overrun Displays in Heating mode
S 07 Heating mode: Pump overrun Displays in Heating mode
S 08 Heating mode: Remaining anti-cycling time xx min Displays in Heating mode
S 20 Warmstart demand Displays in cylinder charging mode
S 22 DHW mode: Pump running Displays in cylinder charging mode
S 31 No heat demand, summer operating mode Special cases
S 34 Heating mode: frost protection Special cases

Live Monitor Menu
