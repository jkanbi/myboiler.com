Vaillant Boiler Burner Modulation Rate

Understanding the burner modulation rate display on your Vaillant boiler and what different power levels indicate about system performance.

Display of the current burner modulation rate (bar graph display)

The boiler modulation rate shows the level that the burner is firing at from minimum to maximum rate. The level that it is at can give some indication as to how the boiler is performing and in some cases can show some sort of fault. In the default setup, the burner rate will ramp to maximum and then start decreasing to a lower level once the return water temperature starts rising ie the heating system require less heat.

If you have installed modulating controls, the modulation rate will be at the level immediately of what is required at the time the room sensor feeds back the current temperature and therefore the required power level, so it could be low or high depending on the timings of the system. If hot water is being heated in combi boilers, usually it will be at maximum power level unless set differently. In stored hot water systems the same principle applies, the power level will usually be maximum unless set otherwise.

Power level is staying low and not reaching the maximum
