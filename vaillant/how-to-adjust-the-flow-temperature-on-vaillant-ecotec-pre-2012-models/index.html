How to adjust the flow temperature on Vaillant Ecotec – Pre 2012 Models

Turn the heating dial to the flow temperature you want. The factory setting is usually 75°C.

Older ecoTECs have four round buttons and two temperature dials.

  1. Turn the heating knob to the flow temperature you want.
