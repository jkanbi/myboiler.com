Vaillant Ecotec (Post 2012) Flow Temperature Adjustment

Video guide showing how to adjust the flow temperature and turn off summer mode on Vaillant Ecotec boilers.

The following video shows you how to adjust the flow temp. Increasing the flow temp also turns off summer mode (umbrella / parasol symbol).

To adjust the flow temp

Newer Ecotecs with 4 rectangle buttons and no dials

  1. Press the top right button shown under the radiator symbol.
  2. Adjust the temperature using the '+' and '-'
  3. Press button under 'OK' ie top right
  4. Press 'back' once to get to home screen.

Additional Resources

If you are unable to adjust the flow temperature or the parasol symbol remains, then you may have a faulty boiler / pcb, that will require a engineer visit to resolve.
