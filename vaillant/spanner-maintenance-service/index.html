Spanner Maintenance Service

Understanding the Vaillant spanner symbol and what it means when your boiler displays the maintenance service icon. Learn how to access the Live Monitor for detailed service information.

Symbol Name Description
Maintenance / Service required In the "Live monitor", you can read further information about the reason for the service

If the open-ended spanner is displayed, the boiler requires a service.

Consult your competent person about doing this. The boiler is not in fault mode but continues to operate.

In the "Live monitor", you can read further information about the reason for the service
