External Flue – Manufacturer Specific Requirements

How much of an external flue can be left white, and whether it can be boxed, depends on the boiler maker.

Intergas

  • Horizontal or vertical: no white showing. No boxing allowed.
  • All components must be accessible.

Worcester Bosch

  • Horizontal or vertical flues: white is allowed to show as long as the joints are siliconed.

Vaillant

  • Horizontal flue: white exposed is ok.
  • Vertical: no white showing.
  • A weather-tight box with an inspection hatch is possibly ok.

Viessmann

  • The white part of the flue is ok to expose because it is UV resistant.
  • 300mm away from the wall for a vertical terminal.
  • On a vertical flue, no white showing.
  • 1m extension on a vertical flue.

Guidance only. Check the current installation manual, and have a Gas Safe registered engineer fit the flue.
