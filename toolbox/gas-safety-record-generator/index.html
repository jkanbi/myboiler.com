Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12)

A free Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12) generator. It fills a one-page LGSR (Form v3.2) in your browser. Only a Gas Safe registered engineer may carry out the checks and issue the record. The engineer who signs it is responsible for its accuracy.

Updated 11 November 2026

Your answers never leave this device. The page does not upload the form, store customer data, or send analytics about what you type. You may optionally keep your own engineer details in this browser.

This tool is for Gas Safe registered engineers. It does not make MyBoiler.com, or anyone named on this site, a Gas Safe registered business.
