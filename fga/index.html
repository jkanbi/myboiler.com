Flue gas analysis

A flue gas analysis is the analyser reading taken in the flue while the boiler is held at maximum or minimum rate. Engineers use it on a service to see whether combustion sits in the band the manufacturer publishes for that model.

The table is the hub reference list, with each reading in its own column. Empty cells were empty on that list. The brand tools check a reading against the limits already published for that brand. The instruction for the appliance in front of you is the one that counts. Opening the case and adjusting combustion is Gas Safe work.
