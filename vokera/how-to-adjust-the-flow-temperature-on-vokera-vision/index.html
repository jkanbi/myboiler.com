How to adjust the flow temperature on a Vokera Vision

Long oval panel: clock display on the left, two knobs, pressure gauge on the right. The heating temperature selector sets the flow to the radiators.

Set the heating flow

On the Vision C user instructions, standard central heating can be set from 40°C to 80°C. The display shows the temperature as you turn the selector. Typical target is about 55°C if the house still warms.

  1. Turn the mode selector to heating and hot water. That is the position where the boiler answers a heating request from the time clock or programmer. Hot water only leaves heating off. Off / standby shows “- -” on the display.
  2. Rotate the heating temperature selector clockwise to increase the heating outlet temperature, or anticlockwise to decrease it, until the display shows the flow you want.
  3. Leave the hot-water temperature selector if you only came to lower the heating. That knob is domestic hot water, 37°C to 60°C, not the radiator flow.

Look up your exact model on Boiler Manuals if the fascia does not match. Then return to the Vokera flow-temperature page or the Boiler Optimiser.
