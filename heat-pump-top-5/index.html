Heat pump top 5

These five brands are the hub list of heat pumps voted for by some experts and experienced installers. The list is names only. It does not include scores, models, or test results.

  1. Nibe
  2. Vaillant
  3. Samsung and Mitsubishi
  4. Grant
  5. Daikin

The hub wrote Mitsubishi as Mitsibushi. This page uses the manufacturer spelling. Samsung and Mitsubishi were one line on that list.

Whether a house suits a heat pump is a separate question from the brand. Start with the heat pump checker and the heat pumps guide. Installing one is a qualified installer’s job.
