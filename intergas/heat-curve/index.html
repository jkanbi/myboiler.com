Intergas Heat Curve

With an outside sensor fitted, the boiler sets the heating flow from the outside temperature. These four points are the curve.

  • A, factory: Tmax CH 80°C, Tmin CH 25°C, Tmin out −7°C, Tmax out 25°C.
  • B, example: Tmax CH 60°C, Tmin CH 25°C, Tmin out −7°C, Tmax out 25°C.

Below Tmin out the flow stays at Tmax. Above Tmax out it stays at Tmin. Between those outside temperatures the flow slides down the line. On an HRE or ECO RF, Tmax is the heating value set on the display.

HRE and ECO RF

  • Tmax CH — set on the display. Highest heating flow, used at Tmin out and colder.
  • Tmin CH — parameter 5. Lowest heating flow, used at Tmax out and warmer.
  • Tmin out — parameter 6. Outside temperature at the left-hand knee, where the flow reaches Tmax.
  • Tmax out — parameter 7. Outside temperature at the right-hand knee, where the flow falls to Tmin.

Xclusive and Xtreme

  • Tmax CH — parameter 50, and the display. Highest heating flow, used at Tmin out and colder.
  • Tmin CH — parameter 51. Lowest heating flow, used at Tmax out and warmer.
  • Tmin out — parameter 52. Outside temperature where the flow reaches Tmax.
  • Tmax out — parameter 53. Outside temperature where the flow falls to Tmin.

These are installer parameters. Confirm the numbers in the manual for the boiler in front of you before changing them. Gas work stays with a Gas Safe registered engineer.
