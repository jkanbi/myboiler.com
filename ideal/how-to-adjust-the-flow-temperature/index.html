How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Ideal Boiler

Ideal Logic panels come in two common layouts. Vogue and Logic Max are separate families. Pick the picture that matches yours. Typical target is about 55°C if the house still warms.

Logic, Logic+

MODE knob on the right. The radiator MIN–MAX knob is heating.

Logic 2

Heating knob on the far right and no MODE knob.

Logic Max / Vogue Max

Later Max boilers use a different digital fascia. No MyBoiler how-to yet — look it up on Boiler Manuals.

Vogue / Independent / Evo

Older Vogue and Independent fascias are not the Logic layout. Look those up on Boiler Manuals.

kW size does not change how you set the flow.

When the flow is set, go back to the Boiler Optimiser to log what it was and what you set.
