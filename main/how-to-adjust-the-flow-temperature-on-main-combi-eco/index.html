How to Adjust the Flow Temperature on a Main Combi Eco

Set the heating flow with the radiator knob. The video takes a Main Combi Eco from 75°C down to 55°C.

This is the older Combi Eco with two grey knobs and a red display. It is not the later Eco Compact.

  1. Leave the left selector on heating and hot water.
  2. Turn the radiator knob until the display shows the flow you want. The range is 25°C to 80°C.
  3. The tap knob is hot water. Leave that if you only came to lower the heating.
