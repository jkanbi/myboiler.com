Formula

Ratio = Electricity ÷ Gas
Breakeven COP = Ratio × Boiler efficiency
Saving % = ((Heat-pump COP − Breakeven COP) ÷ Breakeven COP) × 100

Unit rates must be the same basis (usually £/kWh). Boiler efficiency is a fraction (0.9 = 90%). A heat pump cheaper than gas needs a seasonal COP above the breakeven figure.

Important: This is a unit-rate comparison only. It does not include standing charges, heat-loss, emitter temperature, or installation cost. Information is guidance; gas work needs a Gas Safe engineer.
