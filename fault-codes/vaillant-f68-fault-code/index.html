Vaillant F68 Fault Code

Unstable flame signal

Fault codes / Vaillant

Gas Safe: Gas work, flue work, and PCB replacement on a gas boiler must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Homeowners can check obvious items such as heating pressure, condensate overflow, and whether a control is unplugged — then call an engineer.

Always confirm the part number against the boiler GC number and the installation manual before ordering.

Overview

F.68 is a flame that will not sit still on the ionisation circuit: gas, air ratio, condensate, electrode, or recirculation. It is the “running” cousin of F.28 / F.29.

Vaillant meaning: Unstable flame signal

Possible causes

  • Air in the gas or low inlet pressure
  • Wrong air ratio / restrictor / gas family
  • Blocked condensate route
  • Ionisation lead or electrode intermittent
  • Flue-gas recirculation (flue joint, terminal, or heat exchanger)

Possible solutions

  1. Check meter and appliance working pressure. Purge if the supply was off.
  2. Clear condensate trap, siphon, and outlet. Frozen or blocked condensate is a very common F.68 / F.28 pair.
  3. Inspect and replace the electrode and lead if oxidised or loose.
  4. Check flue joints and the heat-exchanger / burner door seal for recirculation.
  5. Combustion analyse and set CO/CO₂ to the table. Wrong restrictor or gas family also shows as F.93.

Possible parts needed

  • Electrode 090709 or 0020133816 Buy on Amazon Buy on eBay
  • Ignition lead
  • Condensate trap / siphon
  • Burner door gasket / flue seals
  • Gas valve or restrictor only if combustion cannot be set

See F.28 and F.29.
