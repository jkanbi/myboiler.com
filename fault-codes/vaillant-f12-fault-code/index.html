Vaillant F12 Fault Code

Short circuit at cylinder charging sensor (NTC) — only in combination with F.91

Fault codes / Vaillant

Gas Safe: Gas work, flue work, and PCB replacement on a gas boiler must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Homeowners can check obvious items such as heating pressure, condensate overflow, and whether a control is unplugged — then call an engineer.

Always confirm the part number against the boiler GC number and the installation manual before ordering.

Overview

F.12 is the short-circuit counterpart of F.02: the actoSTOR charging NTC (or its cable) is shorted to itself or to earth. Only valid with F.91.

Vaillant meaning: Short circuit at cylinder charging sensor (NTC) — only in combination with F.91

Possible causes

  • Charging NTC internally shorted
  • Cable trapped, chafed, or shorted to the casing
  • Water in a connector

Possible solutions

  1. Unplug the charging NTC. If F.12 clears, the sensor or its lead is shorted.
  2. Inspect the lead along the case and heat exchanger. Replace damaged insulation rather than taping it as a long-term fix.
  3. Replace the NTC, then the lead, then actoSTOR electronics if the short remains on the board input.

Possible parts needed

  • actoSTOR charging NTC
  • NTC cable / harness
  • actoSTOR electronics module
